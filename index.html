About Kick's Crafts

Kick's Crafts is a small, owner-run woodshop. We make some pieces from scratch, like our oak whiskey smokers, and personalize others, like our cutting boards, with your logo. Everything is engraved, finished and packed in our own shop, and we sell at craft fairs.

About Kick's Crafts

Kick's Crafts is a small, owner-run woodshop. We make many pieces from scratch, like our oak whiskey smokers and walnut cutting boards, and personalize others, like our olive wood spoon rests, with your logo. Everything is engraved, finished and packed in our own shop, and we sell at craft fairs.
